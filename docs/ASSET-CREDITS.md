# Asset credits

This is an educational Cake Galerie pet café concept. It is not the official website of Cake Galerie and is not affiliated with Studio Ghibli. All café products, prices, pet profiles, availability, and orders are demonstration data.

## Original film stills

The JPEG files below were downloaded from Studio Ghibli's official film-still galleries on 16 October 2026. They are genuine published film stills; no AI images were generated. The image files are unmodified. The website uses CSS cropping to fit its layouts.

Studio Ghibli's gallery notice allows use within the bounds of common sense. This is not a public-domain or open-source license. Copyright belongs to the credited rights holders. Inclusion in this educational project does not grant additional commercial rights or imply endorsement. Review the original source notice for any future use.

The menu images illustrate the atmosphere; they do not depict the actual products being sold. The cat images are film characters used for fictional sample pet profiles, not photographs of real café animals. Administrators can replace menu and pet images with their own JPG, PNG, or WebP file uploads.

## Mapping software and data

- [Leaflet 1.9.4](https://leafletjs.com/) is vendored unchanged in `public/assets/vendor/`. Its BSD 2-Clause license is included as `public/assets/vendor/LICENSE.txt`. The marker and layer icons ship with Leaflet.
- Map tiles and map data: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), © OpenStreetMap contributors. Attribution appears on the map. Public tile use is subject to the [tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/).
- Address lookup: [Nominatim](https://nominatim.org/), subject to the [public service usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/).
- Route estimates: [OSRM](https://project-osrm.org/). The public demonstration routing service has no service guarantee.

Public mapping services require an internet connection and may enforce availability and usage limits. Use an appropriate hosted provider or your own services for substantial traffic.

## Interface artwork

Small navigation icons (paws, cups, bags, and map pins) are drawn directly as SVG interface symbols. The favicon uses the user-supplied logo illustration described below.

## Supplied logo illustration

The Totoro line drawing was supplied by the user as `download (1).jpg`. Its original artist and source were not supplied. The original drawing is embedded unchanged inside SVG presentation wrappers; an SVG filter makes the background transparent and colors the lines moss green. `logo-lockup.svg/.png` adds the Cake Galerie wordmark; `logo-mark.svg/.png` and `favicon.svg` use the drawing as a mark. No AI-generated imagery is used. Character rights remain with their respective rights holders; no Studio Ghibli affiliation is claimed.
